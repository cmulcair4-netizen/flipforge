Operating playbook

€200 bankroll rule

Keep at least €60–€80 liquid. Initial sweet spot: €40–€120 purchases. Increase deal size only after realised sales.

40%+ target ROI

Use 40% as a filter, not a guarantee. A quick €50–€70 profit can be better than a slow €150 theoretical margin.

Authenticity & ownership

Ask for model/serial details and proof of ownership where appropriate. Walk away from suspicious or counterfeit-looking goods.

Zero-work preference

Working, clean, identifiable items first. No repair projects until the bankroll is substantially larger.

Negotiation

Offer a specific cash/collection price and be prepared to walk away. Never reveal your expected resale margin.

Compound

Reinvest realised profit progressively: €200 → €300 → €450 → €700 → €1,100 → €2,000, rather than betting the entire float on one deal.

⚡ Hourly Scan: an automated hourly deal scan is enabled. Cork is the priority; strong Ireland-wide opportunities are also considered. The scanner uses searchable/public listings and does not assume access to closed or private marketplace data.
